TIERLLAMA LOGO CONCEPTS - for image-generator refinement
Style target: stamp-style, single color, flat vector, no gradients, clean
geometry, reads at small sizes (taskbar/favicon). Blue in drafts = placeholder
color (can be Jev red #E4573D).

A2 - FULL-BODY LLAMA, TIER-BAR LEGS
Full-body llama standing in side profile, facing right. The body is one solid
silhouette; the legs are three rounded vertical bars of slightly different
heights, evoking a rising bar-chart / tiered stack. The neck rises from the
front leg with a small head, one visible ear swept back, tiny white eye dot.
Overall reads as "a llama built from data tiers." Minimal, flat, single
color on transparent background.

B2 - APP-BADGE LLAMA, TIERS AS THE NECK (assistant favorite)
Rounded-square badge (app-icon shape), solid fill. Inside, in knocked-out
negative space: three rounded vertical bars of ascending height, side by
side like an ascending bar chart; from the tallest bar a stylized llama head
and ear emerge, facing right. Concept: "tiers rising into a llama" - the
product is model tiers, the animal is the llama. Reads perfectly as a
taskbar/app icon. Single color + negative space, no gradients.

C - SPEECH-BUBBLE LLAMA (the router that answers)
A solid filled chat speech bubble with a tail at bottom-left. Inside the
bubble, four knocked-out vertical rounded bars of varied heights form an
abstract llama neck/head group (bars read as both a llama neck and a signal
strength / tier indicator). One small llama-ear shape accents the top of the
tallest bar. Concept: Tierllama answers - routing as conversation. Flat,
single color + white interior.

C2 - COIN STAMP PROFILE
Circular coin/stamp mark: thick ring border, inside is a llama neck-and-head
in profile facing right, minimal shapes - neck as a tapering solid column,
head as a small rounded mass, one upright ear, subtle white eye. The most
"classic seal/stamp" of the five. Works great embossed, engraved, or as a
favicon.

D - MINIMAL HEAD IN ROUNDED SQUARE
Rounded-square outline (not filled), inside a minimal llama head-and-neck
glyph: a single curved neck stroke widening into a simple head with one ear
and a negative-space eye. The most restrained/mark-like of the five - closest
to how modern tech logos are drawn (think GitLab/GitHub-adjacent simplicity).
Single color, generous negative space.

NOTES FOR REFINEMENT
- Keep it ONE color, flat - no shading, no gradients, no 3D
- The animal must remain unmistakably a LLAMA (long neck, small head, swept
  ear) - the differentiator vs a camel is the ears; vs Ollama's mark, the
  pose and the tier-bar motif
- Should read at 16px (taskbar) and 512px (store listing)
- Transparent background
